Sean Greaves

Info Portfolio

Sean Greaves is a technologist based in London. He works at the Autonomy Institute, a think tank developing data-driven tools for sustainable economic planning. He joined in 2023 to lead the institute's practical exploration of generative AI.

He previously worked in Applied AI at IBM and Seldon and developed software for investigating corporate networks at Bellingcat. He holds degrees in Mechanical Engineering and Design Informatics from the University of Edinburgh.
