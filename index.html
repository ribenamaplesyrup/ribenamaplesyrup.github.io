Sean Greaves

Sean Greaves is a technologist based in London. He is an Associate Director of Applied AI at Moody's Analytics.

He previously held roles at IBM, Seldon and the Autonomy Institute. He holds degrees in Mechanical Engineering and Design Informatics from the University of Edinburgh, and held fellowships at Bellingcat and the Strelka Institute.
