Sean Greaves

Sean Greaves is a technologist based in London. He is an Associate Director of Applied AI at Moody's Analytics.

He previously led the Autonomy Institute's practical exploration of generative AI, worked in Applied AI at IBM and Seldon, and developed software for investigating corporate networks at Bellingcat. He holds degrees in Mechanical Engineering and Design Informatics from the University of Edinburgh.
