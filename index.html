Sean Greaves is a technologist and researcher based in London.

He works at the Autonomy Institute, a think tank developing data-driven tools for sustainable economic planning. Since mid-2023, he has led the institute's exploration of Large Language Models as an accelerant for progressive actors. During this period he developed a map of Deliveroo's UK dark kitchens, an interactive index to Project 2025, a dataset of AI-related updates in risk disclosure across the S&P 500, a network visualisation of Reform UK's colorful corporate associations, a catalogue of UK businesses reporting financial upside from conflict and a forthcoming weekly newsletter exploring climate risk to small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs).

Prior to Autonomy he developed software for uncovering networks within British corporate filings that has since been used by journalists at Bellingcat and the Financial Times. He holds degrees in Mechanical Engineering and Design Informatics from the University of Edinburgh and previously worked in Applied AI at IBM and Seldon.
