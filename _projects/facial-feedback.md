The following portraits are generated from dozens of images of Bella Hadid, Rihanna and Kim Kardashian’s doppelgängers. I used dlib (C++ machine learning library) to detect each celebrity’s facial landmarks and used these constellations of coordinates as a focal lens to fine-tune and average the faces of their doppelgängers into a collective identity.

 This small project arose from my interests in the aesthetic intersections of doppelgängers within science fiction and religion, celebrity culture and social media induced feedback cycles. I am interested in further exploring how platforms mediate monocultures and outliers.
