[Sugartrail](https://github.com/bellingcat/sugartrail) is an open-source network analysis tool to empower OSINT researchers to navigate corporate ownership networks using data from the UK Companies House register. The tool was developed within a self-directed 2-month tech fellowship awarded by the investigative journalism group [Bellingcat](https://www.bellingcat.com/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} and funded by the Alfred Landecker Foundation.
