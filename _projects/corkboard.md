The internet has made one-man intelligence agencies of all of us. Armed with some names and too much free time, digging around and connecting the dots is a relatively frictionless if time-consuming experience. On occasion, you might find something interesting. Yet it is common to run into hard scaling limits when attempting to map the connections between entities within large networks.

![Report](/images/projects/corkboard/cover-alt-2.png)

To address this, I developed a pipeline that combines web scraping with LLMs to identify passages of online text that evidence meaningful connection between two entities. The output of the pipeline is then stored within an Obsidian vault. 

![Report](/images/projects/corkboard/ref.png){: .img-half}{: .shadow}

At the Autonomy Institute we have put this capacity to work on a range of projects including a collaboration with the [world’s largest trade union federation](https://www.ituc-csi.org/corporate-underminers-of-democracy-en){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}.
