Two years after the launch of ChatGPT, AI is used in some capacity by most major companies. Its rapid adoption brings new risks, some of which remain poorly understood.

I authored the following report examining changes to the “Risk Factors” sections of all S&P 500 10-K filings between 2024 and 2025 to understand how AI-related risks are being described. I draw particular attention to subtle revisions in reporting which are easy to miss in dense legal language but often indicate shifting priorities and emergent anxieties.

![Update to Salesforce’s 10-K Risk Factors](/images/projects/their-capital-at-risk/diff.png){: .img-three-quarters}{: .shadow }

I used textual processing algorithms and large language models (LLMs) to align and compare filings, and created an interactive dataset to explore the results.
