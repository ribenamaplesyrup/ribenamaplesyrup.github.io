Scribe is an AI-powered research tool that dramatically speeds up the analysis of free-form text, transforming weeks of work into actionable insights in minutes. Unlike generic text analysis tools, Scribe is purpose-built for the think tank and public policy sector, by researchers from the sector.

Qualitative data from surveys, interviews, and focus groups is rich in insight but notoriously hard to analyse. Current methods are slow, manual, and prone to error, often leaving valuable data untapped.

Leveraging advanced language models, Scribe transforms large-scale text responses into actionable data by summarising, categorising, and embedding them for seamless navigation between macro-level topics and micro-level perspectives. I developed the initial prototype for Scribe using data from the world’s largest shorter working time trial co-led by the Autonomy Institute.
