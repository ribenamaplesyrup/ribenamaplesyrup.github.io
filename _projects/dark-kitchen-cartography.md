Dark kitchens are dedicated sites for preparing delivery-only takeaway meals; a model that has been widely adopted across the food service industry throughout the past decade. Companies make use of extensive urban datasets and algorithmic techniques to optimise the placement of these sites in well-connected non-residential urban locations where overhead and operating costs are minimal. A better understanding of the successes and failures of dark kitchens could provide insights relevant to economic planning, risk management, urban design and food distribution.

![Screenshot of the interactive map](/images/projects/dark-kitchen-cartography/map.png)

To address the lack of detailed data on the growth and current state of dark kitchens across the UK I developed the first open map of Deliveroo’s UK dark kitchen estate. The data was acquired through scraping takeaway listing data from locations across each of the UK’s several thousand postcode districts.
