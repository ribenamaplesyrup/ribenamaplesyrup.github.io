  • Curation Cartography


    For several years internet radio has taken over as my primary means for discovering new music. I find the format to be more surprising and taste-shifting in comparison to alternatives like Spotify that rely heavily on recommendation algorithms. The experience of transitioning from a state of disorientation to recognition of an artist or DJ’s style over several episodes is addictive. I have been prototyping ways in which this metadata rich format could further differentiate as a uniquely valuable engine for music discovery.

    I have begun developing software to visualise how different shows from NTS Radio are connected through record labels. Record labels are powerful curators yet often remain obscured from the listener. Processing hundreds of hours of mixes to visualise the curatorial pathways, opens up new possibilities for discovering artists, collectives and scenes that might otherwise remain submerged in the endless stream of sound.
