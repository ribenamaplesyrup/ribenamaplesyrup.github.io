  • Curation Cartography


    For many years internet radio has been my favourite way of discovering new music. I find the format to be more surprising and taste-shifting in comparison to alternatives, leading me to consider how this metadata rich format could further differentiate as a uniquely valuable engine for music discovery.

    I have begun developing software to visualise how different shows from NTS Radio are connected through record labels. Labels are powerful curators yet often remain distant from the listener. Processing hundreds of hours of transmission to visualise the curatorial pathways can open up new possibilities for discovering collectives and undercurrents that might otherwise remain submerged in the continuous stream of sound.
