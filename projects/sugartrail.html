  • Sugartrail


    Sugatrail is a tool for building and visualising corporate ownership networks using data from the UK Companies House register. This project was developed within a tech fellowship awarded by the investigative journalism group Bellingcat and funded by the Alfred Landecker Foundation.



    My initial motivation to develop this tool came through my fascination with the cluster of American candy stores on post-pandemic Oxford Street that have recently been investigated for tax evasion and sale of counterfeit products. Journalists at the Financial Times analysed Companies House filings for many of these mysterious companies and found them to exist as a loose ownership network with officers moving between companies and companies shifting between a number of common addresses. The complexity and scale of these networks coupled with the lack of relevant tools for analysing them led me to begin prototyping.





    Sugartrail enables users to build networks of companies, officers and addresses connected within x-degrees of seperation. The tool can build networks that extend from a single company whilst also checking to see if two companies are connected within x-degrees of seperation.
