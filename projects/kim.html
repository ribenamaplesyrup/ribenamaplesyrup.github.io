Facial Feedback



Some faces go viral, inspiring many to undergo different kinds of digital and cosmetic augmentation.

The following portraits are generated from dozens of images of Bella Hadid, Rihanna and Kim Kardashian’s doppelgängers. Dlib (C++ machine learning library) was used to detect each celebrity’s facial landmarks and the resulting constellations of coordinates were treated as a focal lens to fine-tune and average the faces of each doppelgänger into a collective identity.




This small project arose from my interest in the aesthetic intersections of doppelgängers within science fiction and religion, celebrity culture and social media induced feedback cycles.
