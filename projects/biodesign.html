  • Biodesign for Social Impact


    In 2017 I worked alongside two other designers to research the current state of synthetic biology in the UK and explored the potential impact of post-Brexit deregulation on the adoption of DIY labs. We developed speculative case studies detailing how different actors impacted by industrial pollution, gentrification and intellectual property theft might utilise the affordances of emerging biotechnologies within counter strategies. The project was presented at MOMA NYC as part of the 2017 Biodesign Challenge symposium and exhibited at the School of Visual Arts in the form of images, an interactive timeline and artefacts.
