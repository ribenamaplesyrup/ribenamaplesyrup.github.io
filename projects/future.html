    • Shared Futures


    r/Futurology is a popular subreddit for sharing articles on emerging technologies. I was curious to explore what years are discussed most within articles shared in the subreddit since its inception in 2012.

    The Guardian is one of the most popular sources for articles shared in the subreddit. I scraped thousands of articles shared from the Guardian and parsed dates from the articles using the Stanford NLP library. The heat map below references the years mentioned within each article against the date the article was shared. Click on a location within the heatmap to view the reference articles.
